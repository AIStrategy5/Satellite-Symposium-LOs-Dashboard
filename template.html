Method & confidence. Scope: rolling last 12 months (Aug 2025 – Aug 2026); the same window is applied to competitors and to Decera, so the comparison is like-for-like. Only activities whose learning objectives were read verbatim from a live, linked source are included — nothing paraphrased, inferred, or behind a block. Each of the 20 competitor activities and Decera's own activities was individually re-fetched on Aug 7, 2026; all links resolved and all objective text matched. A small number of near-term activities already announced are marked Upcoming. Where a source did not itself name a commercial supporter, the supporter is listed as "not stated on source" rather than asserted. Confidence in the accuracy of every included item: ≥0.97 (each is link-verified). Coverage: this cycle swept ~68 commercial medical-education companies (the full known universe of grant-funded CME providers relevant to you) — the complete list, in four buckets, is at the bottom.
